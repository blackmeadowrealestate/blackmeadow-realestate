Living in the Hudson Valley

I’ve lived in the Hudson Valley since 1998 — 28 years now. I’ve watched the area change, and I’ve also seen how uniquely so many of the homes up here are built.

A place I’ve known for 28 years

I’ve lived in the Hudson Valley since 1998 — 28 years now. This is home. Over that time, the area has changed, the way any place does when you stay long enough to notice. Towns feel different than they did when I first got here. Roads get busier. New people arrive, older places get new uses, and some corners you used to know by heart aren’t quite the same.

You don’t see all of that in a single season. You see it because you’ve been here through the years, and you remember how it used to feel.

Homes that are built in their own way

What hasn’t changed is how unique so many of the homes up here are. They aren’t built like the houses you see everywhere else. A lot of them were put together in very particular ways — custom work, unusual layouts, details that only make sense once you understand the house itself. Those properties don’t explain themselves in a standard listing.

It takes a special kind of real estate agent to market and sell a home like that. You have to be able to see how it was built, why it was built that way, and how to tell that story so the right buyer understands what they’re looking at. That’s the work I want to do here, with Joseph Baratta and Company.
