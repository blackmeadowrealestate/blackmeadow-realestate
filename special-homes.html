Some homes need more than a standard listing

Dominic helps owners present distinctive properties with the care and context they deserve.

Homes that stand apart

A special home — a custom build, a property with history, unusual land, or details you won’t find on every street — shouldn’t be listed like a typical house. Buyers need to understand why it feels different, and sellers need someone who can see that and say it clearly.

Because of his construction and contracting background, Dominic can point to the work behind the walls as well as the first impression: how a space was built, what was chosen on purpose, and how those choices should show up in the listing.
