About

From the inside out

Before helping people buy and sell, Dominic came up through construction and general contracting. That background is the foundation of how he looks at a house — not just the photos and the asking price, but the structure, the systems, and the work that went into it.

He can walk a buyer or a seller through a home from the inside out: how it was built, what to pay attention to, and how to talk about the details that make a special property stand out.
