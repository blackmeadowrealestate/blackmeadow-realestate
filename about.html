Background

Construction and general contracting

Dominic’s work in real estate is shaped by a background in construction and general contracting. He learned houses by building and overseeing them — framing, finishes, and the practical decisions that turn a plan into a place people actually live.

That means he can explain a home from the inside out. When a buyer walks through a special property, or a seller is getting ready to list, he can talk through how the house is put together, what those details are worth, and how to present them clearly — not just as features on a flyer, but as the real story of the home.
