Seller FAQ

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on the home, the price, and the time of year. Before anything goes on the market, we’ll talk through what’s realistic for your property.

My home has a well, septic, or an older addition. Does that matter?

Buyers will ask about them, so it helps to have records ready — well water tests, septic service, and any permits for past work. I can help you pull that together.

Are you a home inspector?

No. But my construction and contracting background helps me spot things a buyer’s inspector is likely to notice, so there are fewer surprises once you have an offer.

What does the free photography include?

Every home I list gets complimentary drone photography of the house and property from above.
