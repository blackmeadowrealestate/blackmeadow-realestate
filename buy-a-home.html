How we look at a house together

Buying a home is easier when someone can explain what you’re walking through. Because of his construction and general contracting background, Dominic can talk through how a house was built, what to pay attention to, and which details are worth a closer look — especially on a distinctive property.

Step 1

Start with what you need

Tell me what you’re looking for, what matters most, and any homes already on your mind. We can use that conversation to narrow the search.

Step 2

Walk the property carefully

On a showing, we look at the house from the inside out — not only the first impression, but the work behind the walls and the choices that shape how it lives.

Step 3

Move forward with clear next steps

If it feels right, we talk through the details and the path from there. This is a starting outline; we can add local process notes before the site goes live.
